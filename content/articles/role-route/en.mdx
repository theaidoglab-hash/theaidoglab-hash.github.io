> Core judgement: do not begin with “Which AI title has the best future?” Begin with what the role must deliver each week and what evidence lets an employer inspect that ability.

## One title can hide four different jobs
AI Engineer is not a standardised job in Australia. One team may expect model experimentation. Another needs product integration. A third needs deployment, permissions and monitoring. A forward-deployed role may combine discovery, implementation and adoption with a customer team. Learning from the title alone produces a long tool list but weak role evidence.

Circle the verbs in the job description. `train`, `experiment` and `benchmark` usually point toward Data/ML. `build`, `integrate` and `ship` suggest AI Application work. `deploy`, `operate`, `secure` and `monitor` point toward Platform/Ops. `discover`, `scope`, `implement` and `adopt` are common in FDE-shaped work. This is a reading aid, not a rigid taxonomy.

## Classify the deliverable
- Data/ML evidence: data decisions, baselines, experiment design, metrics and error analysis.
- AI Application evidence: APIs, product flows, retrieval, tests, latency and failure handling.
- Platform/Ops evidence: CI/CD, permissions, observability, cost, reliability and incident response.
- FDE evidence: discovery, technical scope, prototyping, production handoff and adoption.

A role can span two groups. Find the dominant output and the risk an interviewer is most likely to probe. A JD mentioning both RAG and Kubernetes does not mean you must master two ecosystems first. If the responsibility is shipping an internal capability, application evidence may be more useful than another model-training notebook.

## Work backwards from three real JDs
Choose three roles for which you meet the basic eligibility conditions. For each responsibility, record three things: what you have done, what an interviewer can inspect, and the smallest verifiable project that could close the gap. Select one gap that recurs across all three descriptions.

If evaluation and deployment recur, deepen an existing chatbot. Add a fixed test set, failure taxonomy, permission boundary and reproducible deployment. That creates stronger role evidence than building a fourth chatbot with a different framework.

## Boundaries
A job ad is not the full job. Seniority, work rights, location and team maturity still require separate checks. A portfolio cannot remove a visa or experience requirement. Its job is to provide inspectable material—not guarantee an interview.

## Your next move
Download the role comparison worksheet and map three current JDs. If you still have more than five “highest-priority” gaps, the target is too broad. Narrow the role before beginning another project.
