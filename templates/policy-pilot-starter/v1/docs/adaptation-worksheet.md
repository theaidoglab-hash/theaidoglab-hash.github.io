# Adaptation worksheet

Use this before changing the fixture. Leave a field blank rather than inventing a fact.

## 1. Decision and boundary

- What exact decision is the system allowed to prepare, and what decision must remain human-owned?
- Who owns the policy or source material?
- Which external actions are prohibited even if a draft looks convincing?
- What should happen when there is no current eligible source?

## 2. Data and source control

- Is every field authorised for this use, and is it available at the decision time?
- How are approval status, effective dates, owners, and versions recorded?
- What source changes invalidate a previous test result?
- What data must never appear in a trace, evaluation set, repository, or screenshot?

## 3. Evaluation and release

- What is the manual baseline?
- Who labels a draft as supported, unsupported, or unsafe?
- What is the denominator, time window, and acceptance threshold for each metric?
- Which negative cases must pass before any limited trial?
- Who can approve, pause, or roll back the change?

## 4. Portfolio narrative

Describe the work in this order: business decision, current baseline, authority boundary, source rule, positive and negative tests, measured result if one exists, and the work still needed. Do not replace the last two parts with framework names or a polished chat screen.
